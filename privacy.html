Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

What the apps access

The Drive Reminders Chrome extension and Android app request a single Google permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. It lets them read and write files in their own hidden application folder in the signed-in user's Google Drive. They cannot access any other files in Google Drive or any other Google data.

What is stored, and where

  • Reminders (title, optional note, time, repeat setting and done status) are stored in one file, reminders.json, in that hidden folder of your own Google Drive.
  • A copy is kept on your devices: in the extension's local browser storage, and in the Android app's private storage, so reminders ring without a network connection.
  • App settings, such as the snooze length, stay on the device.

What is not collected

There is no server operated by the developer. No data is sent to the developer or to any third party, and there are no analytics, advertising or tracking. Data travels only between your devices and Google Drive, using Google's APIs.

Use of Google user data

Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the reminder features described above. It is not transferred, sold, or used for advertising, and complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • Delete individual reminders in the extension.
  • To remove everything: in Google Drive, open Settings → Manage apps, find Drive Reminders, and choose Delete hidden app data. Then uninstall the extension and the Android app.
  • To revoke access, remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
