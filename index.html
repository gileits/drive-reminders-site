⏰ Drive Reminders

A small personal reminder tool: create reminders in a Chrome extension, and an Android app rings for them at the set time.

How it works

  1. The Chrome extension creates one-time or repeating reminders.
  2. They are stored in one file in the hidden app folder of your own Google Drive. No other server is involved.
  3. The Android app reads that file, sets a local alarm for each reminder, and plays a short sound with a notification that stays until you tap Done or Snooze.

Google account access

Both apps ask for one permission: drive.appdata, described by Google as "See, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive". It covers only the app's own hidden folder. The apps cannot see your other Drive files, email or contacts.
